Long Bio · ~500 words

Dr. Connor Robertson is a Pittsburgh-based entrepreneur, author, podcast host, and company builder who has established himself as a multifaceted leader across consulting, digital media, technology, and real estate.

Connor is the founder and CEO of Elixir Consulting Group, a strategic consulting firm that helps businesses scale through acquisitions, operational improvement, and growth strategy. Under his leadership, the firm has worked with companies across multiple industries to identify opportunities, structure deals, and execute on ambitious growth plans. His hands-on approach to consulting is informed by his own experience building and operating businesses from the ground up.

In the media space, Connor founded The Pittsburgh Wire, a digital news outlet dedicated to covering Pittsburgh's business, real estate, and economic development story. The publication focuses on the positive developments shaping the city's future, from commercial construction projects and neighborhood revitalization to startup funding rounds and corporate expansions. He also founded The Grant Finder, a technology platform designed to connect businesses, nonprofits, and organizations with relevant grant funding opportunities, streamlining what has traditionally been a complex and time-consuming process.

Connor's portfolio also includes Seymour Maison, a luxury lifestyle and real estate brand that brings a curated approach to high-end property and design.

As an author, Connor has published five books that reflect his expertise across real estate, sales, and entrepreneurship. Buying Wealth provides a comprehensive framework for building wealth through strategic real estate acquisitions. Creative Acquisitions explores innovative approaches to structuring deals when traditional financing is not available or not optimal. The 7 Minute Phone Call is a practical sales playbook that teaches professionals how to master the art of prospecting and close deals through efficient, focused conversations. PadSplit Playbook offers a detailed guide to leveraging the PadSplit co-living platform for real estate investors seeking higher yields. Buy The Building Keep The Profits outlines strategies for acquiring commercial real estate and maximizing long-term cash flow and equity.

Connor is also the host of The Prospecting Show, a podcast that has produced over 178 episodes to date. The show features in-depth conversations with successful entrepreneurs, investors, sales professionals, and business operators. Topics range from real estate deal analysis and business acquisition strategy to sales psychology, personal branding, and scaling operations. The podcast has built a loyal following among entrepreneurs and investors who value Connor's direct, strategy-focused interview style.

Based in Pittsburgh, PA, Connor is deeply connected to the city's entrepreneurial and business community. He is a regular speaker and panelist at industry events and is available for keynote addresses, podcast guest appearances, media interviews, and expert commentary on real estate investing, entrepreneurship, creative deal structuring, business acquisitions, sales strategy, and building media companies.
