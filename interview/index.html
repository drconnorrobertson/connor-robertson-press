Interview Guide

Everything a podcast host, journalist, or event organizer needs to prepare for a conversation with Dr. Connor Robertson.

What Makes Connor Unique as a Guest

Operator, Not Just Commentator

Connor doesn't just talk about entrepreneurship -- he runs four companies simultaneously. Every insight comes from live, current experience building and operating businesses across different industries.

Multi-Dimensional Expertise

From real estate deal structuring and tax strategy to sales psychology and media company building, Connor brings cross-domain knowledge that makes conversations unpredictable and valuable.

Author of Five Books

With five published books across different facets of business and investing, Connor has already distilled his thinking into frameworks and stories that translate well to interviews.

178+ Episodes as a Host

As the host of The Prospecting Show, Connor knows what makes a great conversation. He's a natural on the mic, delivers tight answers, and understands pacing and storytelling.

Suggested Interview Questions

These questions are organized by topic. Feel free to mix and match or use as a starting framework for your conversation.

Entrepreneurship & Business

  1. You run four companies at the same time. How do you structure your time and decision-making across all of them?
  2. What made you decide to build multiple businesses rather than going deep on just one?
  3. What's the hardest lesson you've learned as an entrepreneur that you don't see people talking about?
  4. How do you evaluate whether a new business idea is worth pursuing vs. staying focused on what's working?

Real Estate & Investing

  1. Your book "Creative Acquisitions" focuses on non-traditional deal structuring. Can you walk through a deal where creative financing made it possible?
  2. What is the PadSplit model and why did you write an entire book about it?
  3. For someone starting in real estate with limited capital, what strategy would you recommend today?
  4. How do you think about risk when evaluating a real estate deal?

Sales & Prospecting

  1. Your book is called "The 7 Minute Phone Call." What can someone really accomplish in 7 minutes?
  2. What's the biggest mistake you see people make when cold calling or prospecting?
  3. How has your approach to sales evolved after hosting 178+ episodes of The Prospecting Show?

Media & Content

  1. Why did you start The Pittsburgh Wire, and what gap does it fill in the local media landscape?
  2. What have you learned about building a media company that most people get wrong?
  3. How do you use content -- books, podcast, articles -- as a business development tool?

Personal & Big Picture

  1. What's the best piece of advice you'd give to someone in their 20s who wants to build wealth?
  2. If you had to start over with nothing but your knowledge, what would you do first?
  3. Where do you see yourself and your businesses in five years?
  4. What does success look like to you beyond the financial metrics?
