About Dr. Robertson

Dr. Connor Robertson is a Pittsburgh-based entrepreneur, author, and podcast host who has built a portfolio of companies spanning consulting, media, real estate, and technology.

As the author of five published books -- Buying Wealth, Creative Acquisitions, The 7 Minute Phone Call, PadSplit Playbook, and Buy The Building Keep The Profits -- Connor has established himself as a leading voice in real estate investing, creative deal structuring, and entrepreneurial growth.

Through The Prospecting Show, his long-running podcast with over 178 episodes, Connor interviews top performers across business, investing, and sales, delivering actionable insights to a growing audience of entrepreneurs and investors.
